Why this layout

  • The jury reads the poster in about 10 seconds: one big picture, three numbers, a QR.
  • The same cross-section appears in the app and on a slide, so poster, talk and QR feel like one product.
  • The KPI row uses only Dare to Dream results from the report, so it can't contradict the slides.
  • The pipes are drawn wider than standard district-heating pipes. That shows honestly that 5GDHC needs larger diameters, which the teacher asked about.

The QR is a placeholder pattern. The real one would point to the hosted Guro Twin.
